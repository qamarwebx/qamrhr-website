Oil & Gas

The oil and gas industry plays a critical role in the global economy, requiring a highly skilled and adaptable workforce to meet the demands of exploration, drilling, production, and refining.

At Qamr International, we specialize in providing experienced professionals to support operations in this vital sector. Our talent pool includes engineers, rig workers, geologists, project managers, safety officers, and technicians who are trained to handle the complex challenges of the oil and gas industry.

Why Choose Our Oil & Gas Services?

Specialized Talent: Engineers, operators, and technicians trained for critical operations.

Safety Assurance: Personnel adhere to stringent HSE (Health, Safety, Environment) standards.

Flexible Deployment: Staff for both short-term projects and long-term operations.

Cost Optimization: Efficient staffing to reduce operational costs

Key Features
  • Workforce supply for exploration, drilling, and production activities.
  • Expertise in hazardous environment operations.
  • Compliance with international safety and environmental regulations.
  • Training programs for continuous skill enhancement.

Whether it’s offshore drilling, onshore exploration, or downstream refining, we provide personnel capable of supporting every stage of the operation, ensuring safety, efficiency, and compliance with industry regulations. We understand the high-risk nature of the industry, and we ensure that our candidates are equipped with the necessary skills, certifications, and knowledge to meet the stringent health, safety, and environmental standards.

With a strong focus on operational excellence, we help businesses optimize production, maintain safety protocols, and enhance overall efficiency, contributing to long-term success in this competitive sector.
