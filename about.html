About Us

Qamr International,

A Human Resources Consultancy and Manpower Company Based in India

Stands out for its unique approach to Sourcing and Deploying an exceptionally skilled Workforce to esteemed clients. Our commitment to quality, extensive industry knowledge, and a vast network are the pillars of our success.

We work across diverse Gulf sectors, including construction, transportation, oil & gas, and hospitality, providing a seamless hiring process through our advanced Applicant Tracking System (ATS). This system simplifies job submissions and ensures employers receive shortlisted applicants' CVs according to their needs.

Since its establishment in 2005 by Mohammad Qamruddin Khan, Qamr International has been officially registered with the Ministry of External Affairs, Government of India, and the Musaned, Government of the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia.

Mission

Our mission is to deliver top-tier, reliable Manpower Solutions that meet the unique staffing needs of businesses in diverse industries. We are committed to providing exceptional recruitment services, building long-term partnerships, and empowering businesses with the right talent to achieve their objectives.

Vision

Our vision is to become a leading global provider of recruitment and manpower solutions, known for our dedication to excellence and our ability to meet the evolving needs of our clients. We aspire to build a sustainable workforce for companies across the world, supporting their growth and success.

Business Goal

Our business goal is to expand our reach across industries and regions, establishing ourselves as the preferred partner for talent acquisition. We aim to consistently deliver the highest-quality manpower to businesses, ensuring they are equipped with the skilled workforce necessary to thrive in a competitive global market.

Strength

Our strength lies in our deep industry knowledge, extensive network, and ability to match the right talent with the right job. We have a dedicated team of recruitment professionals committed to providing customized staffing solutions that address the specific challenges of each client, ensuring success in every placement.
