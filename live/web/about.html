About Us

Qamr International, a Human Resources Consultancy and Manpower Company based in India

Qamr International, a Human Resources Consultancy and Manpower Company based in India, Renowned for its expertise in sourcing and deploying exceptionally skilled manpower to esteemed clients. We closely collaborate with various sectors, including recruitment agencies, construction, transportation, manufacturing, maintenance, commercial, retail, power, oil & gas, and hospitality, across the Gulf region.

At Qamr International, we serve as a reliable bridge connecting our candidates to promising opportunities, ensuring meticulous attention to their job responsibilities and securing the best salary packages for their professional growth.

Founder of Qamr International by Mohammad Qamruddin Khan, established its roots in 2005 and is officially registered with the Ministry of External Affairs, Government of India, as well as the Musaned, Government of the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia.

Our Mission

At Qamr International, we connect people with great jobs worldwide. We're a trusted agency known for integrity, dependability, and dedication. Through strong employer relationships, we empower individuals and foster growth globally.

Our Vision

At Qamr International, we aspire to be a premier global Company in Human Resource recruitment. Our commitment to achieving this goal is grounded in hard work and the delivery of top-quality services. We prioritise forging robust professional relationships with our clients, ensuring the provision of long-term, dependable services.
